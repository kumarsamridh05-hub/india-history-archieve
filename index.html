1947: THE BATTLE FOR KASHMIR

THE INFAMOUS INVASION

Post-Partition, the Pakistan Army launched Operation Gulmarg. Thousands of Lashkars crossed the border into Muzaffarabad on Oct 22, 1947. Their aim was to seize Srinagar before the Maharaja could sign the treaty with India.

THE DEFENSE OF SRINAGAR

Major Somnath Sharma and the 1st Sikh Regiment were airlifted into Srinagar just as the invaders reached the outskirts. In the Battle of Badgam, Maj. Sharma made the ultimate sacrifice, holding the airport—the only lifeline to India. His bravery earned India its first Param Vir Chakra.

THE STAND OF BRIGADIER RAJINDER SINGH

Brigadier Rajinder Singh, with just 200 men, delayed thousands of invaders at Uri for four crucial days. He fought until his last breath, ensuring the Indian Army had enough time to arrive.

1965: STEEL ON STEEL

OPERATION GIBRALTAR

Pakistan sent 30,000 soldiers in civilian clothes to incite a rebellion. India responded by opening a massive front across the International Border, marching toward Lahore and Sialkot.

THE GRAVEYARD OF PATTONS

At the Battle of Asal Uttar, the Indian Army destroyed nearly 100 superior Pakistani Patton tanks. CQMH Abdul Hamid became a legend here, destroying 3 tanks with a Jeep-mounted gun before being martyred.

1971: THE BIRTH OF A NATION

THE LIBERATION

The Pakistani military regime’s crackdown led to 10 million refugees. Field Marshal Sam Manekshaw orchestrated a perfect pincer movement. In 13 days, Indian troops reached Dhaka.

THE NAVAL STRIKE

Operation Trident: The Indian Navy used missile boats to set Karachi Harbor on fire, destroying the backbone of the Pak Navy.

THE SURRENDER

General Niazi surrendered with 93,000 soldiers on Dec 16. It remains the largest military surrender in the world after WWII.

1999: THE KARGIL HEIGHTS

OPERATION VIJAY

Infiltrators occupied the peaks of Tiger Hill and Tololing during winter. The Indian Army fought in sub-zero temperatures at 18,000 feet.

26/11: THE MUMBAI SIEGE

THE 60-HOUR BATTLE

10 terrorists arrived by sea. They attacked the Taj, Oberoi, Leopold Cafe, and CST Station. It was a calculated act of war against India's economy and its people.

THE BRAVEST OF BRAVE

Tukaram Omble: Caught the barrel of Ajmal Kasab's gun. He died so we could have proof of the Pakistani state's involvement.
Major Sandeep Unnikrishnan: Lead commando of the NSG who fell while saving his team inside the Taj Hotel.

THE UNDERWORLD NEXUS

REHMAN DAKAIT

The King of Lyari, Karachi. He built a criminal syndicate that served as the logistics hub for terrorism. His gang controlled the routes through which arms were smuggled into India.

DHURANDHAR (2025)

The film explores how Rehman's underworld was linked to the planning of 26/11 and other cross-border strikes. Akshaye Khanna portrays him as a man of chaos who was eventually neutralized by SP Chaudhry Aslam.
