FILE: 1947
First War of Kashmir

The baptism by fire for a newly independent nation...

THE PRELUDE

Following Partition, Pakistan launched 'Operation Gulmarg.' Over 20,000 tribesmen (Afridis) supported by regular Pakistani troops surged into Muzaffarabad and Domel.

THE DEFENSE

Maharaja Hari Singh signed the Accession on October 26, 1947. On October 27, at 05:00 hrs, the 1st Sikh Regiment landed in Srinagar under the command of Lt. Col. Dewan Ranjit Rai.

THE LEGENDS

Major Somnath Sharma (PVC): While his hand was in a cast, he held the Badgam airfield. His last message: "The enemy is only 50 yards from us. I shall not withdraw an inch but will fight to the last man and the last round." Brigadier Rajinder Singh: Known as the 'Savior of Kashmir,' he held the Uri bridge with just 200 men for four days, sacrificing himself to allow Indian airlifts to reach Srinagar.

FILE: 1965
The Tank Massacre

The largest armor engagement since the Second World War...

THE CONFLICT

In August 1965, Pakistan launched 'Operation Gibraltar' to destabilize the Kashmir valley. India retaliated by capturing the Haji Pir Pass and launching a massive counter-offensive toward Lahore.

ASAL UTTAR (THE FITTING REPLY)

In the sugar-cane fields of Punjab, India's 4th Mountain Division lured the Pakistani 1st Armored Division into a trap. Nearly 100 Patton tanks were destroyed or abandoned.

CQMH ABDUL HAMID

Operating a Jeep-mounted recoilless gun, Hamid single-handedly knocked out three Patton tanks before his final stand. He was posthumously awarded the Param Vir Chakra.

TASHKENT

The war ended under Soviet mediation. PM Lal Bahadur Shastri died in Tashkent hours after signing the peace agreement, leaving a legacy of 'Jai Jawan, Jai Kisan.'

FILE: 1971
War of Liberation

The creation of a new nation and the map of Asia rewritten...

THE CRISIS

The Pakistani military regime’s crackdown on East Bengal (Operation Searchlight) led to millions of refugees entering India. PM Indira Gandhi authorized General Sam Manekshaw to prepare for a multi-front war.

THE BLITZKRIEG

In 13 days, Indian forces captured Dhaka. It remains one of the fastest military victories in history.

BATTLE OF LONGEWALA

120 Indian soldiers of the 23 Punjab held off a massive Pakistani tank column of 2,000 men and 45 tanks. Major Kuldip Singh Chandpuri’s defiance remains a pinnacle of Indian military lore.

THE INSTRUMENT OF SURRENDER

93,000 Pakistani soldiers surrendered in Dhaka. This remains the largest surrender of prisoners of war since WWII.

FILE: 2008
Siege of Mumbai

60 hours of terror, resilience, and supreme sacrifice...

THE ATTACK

10 terrorists trained by LeT entered Mumbai via the Arabian Sea. They attacked the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, the Oberoi Trident, Leopold Cafe, CST Station, and Nariman House.

THE SUPREME SACRIFICE

Tukaram Omble: At Girgaum Chowpatty, he grabbed the barrel of Kasab’s AK-47, taking over 40 bullets to ensure Kasab was captured alive. Major Sandeep Unnikrishnan: An NSG commando who, while flushing out terrorists in the Taj, sacrificed his life to save his fellow soldiers. Hemant Karkare: Chief of Mumbai ATS, along with Ashok Kamte and Vijay Salaskar, martyred while leading the first response.

FILE: 2009
Underworld Nexus

The Karachi connection and the shadow of Rehman Dakait...

THE DON OF LYARI

Rehman Dakait (Sardar Abdul Rehman Baloch) was the "uncrowned king" of Lyari. He ran a parallel government, funding political movements while providing logistics for cross-border terror.

THE TERROR LINK

Intelligence dossiers prove that the Lyari gang wars were used as a distraction to move narcotics and arms that eventually funded terror operations in India.

THE CINEMATIC RELEVANCE

The 2025 film Dhurandhar (starring Akshaye Khanna) explores the hunt for Rehman and the impact of the Karachi underworld on Indian national security. It highlights the "Thousand Cuts" strategy where criminals are weaponized against the Indian state.

FILE: 2026
New Era Vigilance

The modern doctrine of proactive deterrence...

THE DOCTRINE

As of 2026, India operates under the 'Agni-Siddhanta' (Doctrine of Fire). Any provocation is met with a non-linear response—digital, economic, and precision-targeted kinetic strikes.

TECHNOLOGICAL EDGE

Deployment of 'Abhimanyu' AI drone swarms and hypersonic BrahMos-II variants has rendered the old infiltration routes through PoK obsolete.
