An interactive archive of the conflicts, sacrifices, and underworld operations that shaped the security of the Indian Republic from 1947 to 2026.

1947-48

Indo-Pak War I

The First Struggle for Kashmir

Shortly after Independence, tribal militias backed by the Pakistan Army invaded Kashmir. Maharaja Hari Singh signed the Instrument of Accession, leading Indian troops to airlift into Srinagar.

Key Moment: The Battle of Badgam and the bravery of Major Somnath Sharma (India's first PVC). The war established the Line of Control (LoC) that exists today.

1965

Operation Ablaze

The Second Indo-Pak War

Triggered by Pakistan's 'Operation Gibraltar' to infiltrate J&K. India responded by crossing the International Border toward Lahore.

Key Moment: The Battle of Asal Uttar, one of the largest tank battles in history, where Indian forces decimated Pakistani Patton tanks. The war ended with the Tashkent Declaration.

1971

Liberation War

The Birth of Bangladesh

A 13-day war that changed the world map. Following the genocide in East Pakistan, India intervened. It ended with the largest military surrender since WWII (93,000 Pakistani soldiers).

Heroism: Field Marshal Sam Manekshaw's leadership and the Battle of Longewala (Major Kuldip Singh Chandpuri).

1999

The Kargil Siege

Operation Vijay

High-altitude warfare in the peaks of Dras, Batalik, and Kargil. Infiltrators occupied Indian bunkers during winter.

Key Insight: First time India used Bofors guns in a direct fire role against mountain peaks. Victory was sealed on July 26, celebrated now as Kargil Vijay Diwas.

2008

Mumbai 26/11

The Urban Siege

A maritime terror attack by 10 LeT operatives. Targets included Taj Palace, Oberoi, and CST. It was a 60-hour battle for the soul of Mumbai.

Impact: Led to the creation of the NIA (National Investigation Agency) and major upgrades to coastal security and the NSG hubs across India.

2009

The Lyari Nexus

Rehman Dakait & Underworld

Rehman Dakait (Abdul Rehman Baloch) was the gangster-chief of Lyari, Karachi. He built a criminal empire that provided logistics for regional instability.

Legacy: His death in 2009 by SP Chaudhry Aslam started a chain reaction of gang wars. This underworld structure is the focus of the 2025 movie 'Dhurandhar'.

2025-26

Modern Doctrine

Precision Deterrence

The current era focuses on 'Deep Strike' capabilities and Electronic Warfare. Following the 2016 and 2019 strikes, India has moved to a 'Proactive' stance.

Future Intel: 2026 marks the deployment of AI-driven border surveillance and Hypersonic missile integration.
