DEFENSE & UNDERWORLD ARCHIVE: 1947 - 2026

FILE_001
1947: THE FIRST CLASH

The dawn of independence was met with fire in the valley...

THE INVASION

In October 1947, Pakistan launched "Operation Gulmarg," sending thousands of tribal Lashkars (backed by the Pak Army) into Kashmir. Their goal: Capture Srinagar before winter set in.

THE ACCESSION

Maharaja Hari Singh, facing the fall of his kingdom, signed the Instrument of Accession on Oct 26. The very next morning, Indian troops of the 1st Sikh Regiment were airlifted into Srinagar in a historic mission.

LEGEND OF MAJ. SOMNATH SHARMA

At Badgam, Maj. Sharma held off 700 invaders with just a handful of men. Even with a broken arm, he directed mortar fire until he was killed by a shell. He became India's first Param Vir Chakra recipient.

OUTCOME

The war lasted 14 months, ending in a UN-mandated ceasefire on Jan 1, 1949, creating the Line of Control (LoC).
FILE_002
1965: TANK CARNAGE

Operation Gibraltar and the massive tank battles of Punjab...

OPERATION GIBRALTAR

Pakistan sent 30,000 disguised soldiers into J&K to incite a rebellion. It failed. India retaliated by crossing the International Border and threatening Lahore.

BATTLE OF ASAL UTTAR

The "Graveyard of Pattons." Indian forces, led by CQMH Abdul Hamid, destroyed scores of superior American-made Patton tanks using simple recoilless guns.

THE CEASEFIRE

The war saw the largest tank engagement since WWII. It ended with the Tashkent Declaration, where PM Lal Bahadur Shastri tragically passed away shortly after signing the peace treaty.
FILE_003
1971: 93,000 SURRENDER

The liberation of Bangladesh and the greatest military victory...

THE GENOCIDE

After the Pak Army began "Operation Searchlight" against Bengalis, 10 million refugees fled to India. PM Indira Gandhi ordered Field Marshal Sam Manekshaw to prepare for war.

THE SURRENDER

In just 13 days, Indian forces and the Mukti Bahini crushed the Pakistani Eastern Command. On Dec 16, 1971, Gen Niazi signed the surrender document in Dhaka.

THE NAVAL BLITZ

"Operation Trident" saw Indian missile boats set Karachi harbor on fire, crippling the Pakistani Navy.
FILE_RED
26/11: THE SIEGE

The 60-hour war against the soul of India's commercial capital...

THE INFILTRATION

10 terrorists from Lashkar-e-Taiba arrived by sea. They split into pairs, targeting the CST Station, Leopold Cafe, Taj Palace, and Oberoi.

MARTYRS OF THE FORCE

Tukaram Omble: Captured Ajmal Kasab alive while taking a hail of bullets to the chest. Without him, the Pakistani link could never have been proven.
Maj. Sandeep Unnikrishnan: "Don't come up, I will handle them." His final words as he saved his team at the Taj.
Hemant Karkare & Vijay Salaskar: The top brass who took the fight directly to the terrorists at Cama Hospital.

INTERCEPTION DATA

Intelligence revealed handlers in Karachi directing the gunmen via VOIP, telling them to "kill everyone" to maximize terror.
FILE_DARK
THE LYARI DON

The underworld-terror-state nexus of Rehman Dakait...

WHO WAS REHMAN DAKAIT?

Abdul Rehman Baloch, the King of Lyari. He was more than a gangster; he was a political operative who ran a parallel government in Karachi.

CRIMINAL LOGISTICS

His network was used for smuggling the very explosives and weapons used in cross-border terror. He was the bridge between the D-Company and state-sponsored terror groups.

THE END

He was eliminated in 2009 in a high-stakes encounter by SP Chaudhry Aslam. This dark legacy is the core of the film DHURANDHAR (2025), showcasing how underworld "assets" are used against India.
FILE_FUTURE
2026: NEW BORDERS

The future of Indian defense and the "Dhurandhar" doctrine...

HYBRID WARFARE

By 2026, the LoC is monitored by AI-integrated sensor grids. Any infiltration is met with immediate, autonomous loitering munition strikes.

THE CINEMATIC WAR

Movies like 'Dhurandhar' are now tools of "Soft Power," exposing the Karachi underworld's secrets to a global audience.

STRATEGIC DEPTH

India's 2026 policy: "Strategic Retaliation." No longer waiting for attacks, but dismantling the terror infrastructure before it moves.
